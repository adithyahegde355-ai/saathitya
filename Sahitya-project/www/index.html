      About Sahitya

      Sahitya (ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ) is a free reader for literature and poetry in Kannada, Hindi, English and many other Indian languages. No account, no ads, no payment.

      Where the books come from

      • Wikisource – community-proofread texts in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Odia, Urdu, Nepali and English.
      • Project Gutenberg – free e-books, mostly English plus some Indian-language titles.
      • Internet Archive – scanned books, including many Indian-language titles. Only openly readable scans are listed.

      Good to know

      • Search works best in the language's own script, for example ಬಸವಣ್ಣ or कबीर.
      • Newer books are usually still under copyright, so the catalogue is strongest for classics, devotional poetry and older authors.
      • Copyright rules differ by country. If you see a book that should not be listed, report it to the source site.
      • Use A− / A+ and the ◐ button to change text size and theme while reading.
